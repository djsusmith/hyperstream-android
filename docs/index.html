Architecture & Features

Engineered without compromises.

⚡

128-Thread Segment Halving

Unlike standard download managers capped at 8-16 connections, HyperStream splits single files into up to 128 concurrent chunks, discarding slow network paths automatically to max out 5G and gigabit Wi-Fi.

📲

Universal Share Sheet Flow

Tap "Share" inside YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook. HyperStream intercepts the intent in under 40 milliseconds, resolving direct CDN streams without opening intrusive external web browsers.

🎵

320k Studio MP3 Extractor

Extract uncompressed high-bitrate audio directly from music videos and podcasts with 320 kbps, 192 kbps, or 128 kbps presets, writing ID3 tags and cover art straight to your Android storage.

🚫

Watermark-Free TikTok & Reels

Direct parsing of TikTok's original upstream video buffers strips watermark overlays completely. Supports carousel photo slideshows, music extraction, and Instagram 1080p reels.

🧲

BitTorrent & Debrid Support

Powered by librqbit in native Rust with sequential piece streaming for immediate video playback while downloading, magnetic link parsing, and Real-Debrid / TorBox acceleration.

🛡️

100% Open Source & Ad-Free

GPLv3 certified. No third-party ad networks, no telemetry SDKs, no subscriptions. Built for power users who demand privacy and pure raw performance.
